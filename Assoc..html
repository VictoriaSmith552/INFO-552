American Chemical Society

Recording Secretary & Vice President — DSU Chapter

The American Chemical Society is the world's largest scientific society, dedicated to advancing chemistry and its practitioners. As Recording Secretary and Vice President of the DSU chapter, I supported chapter operations, coordinated events, and advocated for fellow chemistry students at the departmental and institutional level.

Safe Space Strategies

Project Manager

Safe Space Strategies is a consulting firm dedicated to helping organizations build inclusive, equitable, and psychologically safe environments. Through training, facilitation, and organizational development, the company works with institutions to foster cultures where every person feels valued and heard. As Project Manager, I support operations and contribute to initiatives that align closely with my own values around access, equity, and community.

Alliance for Minority Participation

Recording Secretary & Miss AMP — DSU Chapter

The NSF-funded Alliance for Minority Participation supports underrepresented students in STEM fields, promoting academic excellence and access to research opportunities. Serving as both Recording Secretary and Miss AMP, I represented the program publicly while contributing to its administrative functions and outreach efforts at Delaware State University.
