MLIS Course Curriculum

Fall 2025

INFO 590 — Foundations in Data

An introduction to data concepts, structures, and practices relevant to library and information science, including data management, curation, and literacy.

INFO 508 — Design Thinking

An exploration of human-centered design principles and methods applied to information problems, emphasizing empathy, iteration, and creative problem-solving.

Winter 2025

INFO 507 — Leading & Managing Information Organizations

A study of leadership theory, organizational management, and strategic planning as applied to libraries and other information institutions.

INFO 652 — Information Behavior

An examination of how people seek, use, and interact with information, drawing on research models and theories from information science and related fields.

INFO 560 — Intro to Archives I

An introduction to archival theory and practice, including appraisal, arrangement, description, preservation, and access to primary source materials.

Spring 2026

INFO 662 — Metadata and Resource Description

A survey of metadata standards, schemas, and descriptive practices used to organize and provide access to information resources across different contexts and communities.

INFO 552 — Intro to Web Design

An introduction to web design fundamentals including HTML, CSS, and basic principles of usability and accessibility for information professionals.

INFO 650 — Public Library Service

An overview of public library philosophy, services, collections, and community engagement, with attention to equity, access, and the evolving role of the public library.

INFO 501 — Information Professionals, Resources, and Services

A foundational course examining the roles and responsibilities of information professionals across diverse library and information settings.
